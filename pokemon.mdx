## Types

Every Pokémon has one or two types. Types determine which moves are strong or weak against other Pokémon.

| Type | Example Pokémon | Strong against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Charmander | Grass, Ice, Bug, Steel |
| Water | Squirtle | Fire, Ground, Rock |
| Grass | Bulbasaur | Water, Ground, Rock |
| Electric | Pikachu | Water, Flying |
