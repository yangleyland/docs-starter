Pokémon is a media franchise that began with the 1996 video games *Pokémon Red* and *Pokémon Green* in Japan. Trainers catch, train, and battle creatures called Pokémon.

## Types

Every Pokémon has one or two types. A type determines which moves are strong or weak against other Pokémon. For example, Water moves are strong against Fire Pokémon.
