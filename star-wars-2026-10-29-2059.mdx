Star Wars is a science fantasy franchise created by George Lucas. This page covers the franchise's story eras, major factions, and iconic locations.

  This page was created on October 29, 2026 at 20:59 UTC.
</Note>

## Story eras

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prequel era">
    The Republic falls, the Jedi Order is destroyed, and Anakin Skywalker becomes Darth Vader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Original era">
    The Rebel Alliance fights the Galactic Empire and destroys two Death Stars.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sequel era">
    The Resistance fights the First Order, and Rey takes up the Jedi legacy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Major factions

| Faction | Era | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Galactic Republic | Prequel | Governing the galaxy through the Senate |
| Jedi Order | All | Keeping peace using the light side of the Force |
| Galactic Empire | Original | Ruling the galaxy under Emperor Palpatine |
| Rebel Alliance | Original | Restoring the Republic |
| First Order | Sequel | Rebuilding the Empire's power |
| Resistance | Sequel | Opposing the First Order |
