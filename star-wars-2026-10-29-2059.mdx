## Notable planets

| Planet | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| Tatooine | Desert home world of Anakin and Luke Skywalker |
| Hoth | Ice planet and site of a Rebel Alliance base |

| Naboo | Home world of Padmé Amidala |
| Coruscant | Capital of the Galactic Republic and the Galactic Empire |
