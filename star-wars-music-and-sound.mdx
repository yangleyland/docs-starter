Star Wars is a science fantasy franchise created by George Lucas. This page covers the franchise's music and sound design.

## The score

Composer John Williams wrote the score for the nine Skywalker saga films. The London Symphony Orchestra performed the original 1977 soundtrack.

Williams used leitmotifs, which are short musical themes tied to a character, place, or idea. The score reuses and transforms these themes across the films.

## Recurring themes

| Theme | Associated with |
| --- | --- |
| Main Title | The opening crawl and the saga as a whole |
| Force Theme | The Force and Luke Skywalker's hopes |
| The Imperial March | Darth Vader and the Galactic Empire |
| Princess Leia's Theme | Leia Organa |
| Duel of the Fates | Duels with Darth Maul in *The Phantom Menace* |
| Rey's Theme | Rey in the sequel trilogy |

## Sound design

Sound designer Ben Burtt created many of the franchise's signature sounds.

  <Accordion title="Darth Vader's breathing">
