## docs.json

The configuration file at the root of your project. It sets your site's theme, colors, logo, navbar, footer, and navigation. See [Global settings](/essentials/settings).

## Navigation

The structure of links in your site's menu. You define it in the `navigation` field of `docs.json`. New pages don't appear until you add them. See [Navigation](/essentials/navigation).

## Anchor

A top-level navigation entry that groups related sections of your site, such as "Starter Guides" or "Starter API Reference". See [Navigation](/essentials/navigation).

## Frontmatter

The YAML block at the top of an MDX file, between `---` lines. It sets page metadata such as `title`, `description`, and `icon`. See [Markdown syntax](/essentials/markdown).

## Snippet

A reusable piece of content stored in the `snippets` folder. You import it into multiple pages so the content stays in sync. See [Reusable snippets](/essentials/reusable-snippets).
