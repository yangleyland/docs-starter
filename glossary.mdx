## docs.json

The configuration file at the root of your project. It controls your theme, colors, logo, navbar, footer, and navigation. See [Global settings](/essentials/settings).

## Navigation

The structure of groups and pages that appears in the sidebar. You define it in the `navigation` field of `docs.json`. Pages don't appear in the sidebar until you add them there. See [Navigation](/essentials/navigation).

## Anchor

A top-level navigation item that appears at the top of the sidebar, such as **Starter Guides** or **Starter API Reference**. Each anchor contains its own groups and pages.

## Frontmatter

The YAML block at the top of an MDX file, between `---` lines. It sets page metadata such as `title`, `description`, and `icon`. See [Markdown syntax](/essentials/markdown).

## Snippet

A reusable piece of content stored in the `snippets` folder. You import a snippet into any page to keep shared content in sync. See [Reusable snippets](/essentials/reusable-snippets).
