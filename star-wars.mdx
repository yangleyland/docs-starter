Star Wars is a science fantasy franchise created by George Lucas. The first film premiered in 1977 and is set "a long time ago in a galaxy far, far away."

## Core concepts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Force">
    The Force is an energy field that connects all living things. Jedi and Sith draw on it for abilities such as telekinesis and enhanced reflexes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Jedi and Sith">
    The Jedi use the light side of the Force to protect peace. The Sith use the dark side to gain power.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Lightsabers">
    A lightsaber is an energy blade weapon used by Jedi and Sith. Its blade color varies by wielder.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Lightsabers

A lightsaber is a weapon with a plasma blade contained by an energy field. Jedi build their own lightsaber, and a kyber crystal powers the blade.

| Blade color | Typically wielded by |
| --- | --- |
| Blue | Jedi, such as Luke Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi |
| Green | Jedi, such as Yoda and Luke Skywalker |
| Red | Sith, such as Darth Vader |
