The solar system consists of the Sun and everything that orbits it, including planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

## The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of the solar system. It holds more than 99% of the system's mass.

## Planets

The solar system has eight planets. In order from the Sun:

1. Mercury
2. Venus
3. Earth
4. Mars
5. Jupiter
6. Saturn
7. Uranus
8. Neptune
