The solar system consists of the Sun and the objects bound to it by gravity. This includes planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

## The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of the solar system. It contains most of the system's mass and provides light and heat to the planets.

## Planets

Eight planets orbit the Sun. In order of distance from the Sun, they are:
