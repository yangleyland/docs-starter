Star Wars stories also continue on television. Series span the Republic, Imperial, and New Republic eras.

## Animated series

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="The Clone Wars" icon="shield">
    Premiered in 2008. Follows Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Ahsoka Tano during the Clone Wars.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rebels" icon="rocket">
    Premiered in 2014. Follows the crew of the *Ghost* as they fight the Empire.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The Bad Batch" icon="users">
    Premiered in 2021. Follows a squad of experimental clones after the fall of the Republic.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
