Good care keeps dogs healthy and happy. This page covers the basics. For breed information, see [Types of dogs](/dogs).

## Core care areas

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Feeding" icon="bone">
    Feed a balanced diet sized to your dog's age, weight, and activity level.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exercise" icon="person-running">
    Walk and play daily. Energetic breeds need more activity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Grooming" icon="scissors">
    Brush the coat regularly and trim nails as needed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Care by life stage

| Stage | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Puppy | Vaccinations, socialization, basic training |
| Adult | Regular exercise, dental care, annual vet visits |
| Senior | Joint support, softer bedding, more frequent vet visits |
