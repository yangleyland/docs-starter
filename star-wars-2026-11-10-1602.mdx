## Force abilities

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Force push and pull">
    Moves objects or opponents with telekinesis. Jedi and Sith both use this ability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mind trick">
    Influences weak-minded beings to follow suggestions. Obi-Wan Kenobi uses it on Imperial stormtroopers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Force lightning">
    Channels the dark side to shoot lightning from the hands. Emperor Palpatine is known for this ability.
