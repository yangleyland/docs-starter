<Update label="Nov 17, 2026" tags={["Added"]}>
## Glossary, new dog breeds, and daily Star Wars guides

### New features

- **Glossary**: Look up core terms like docs.json, navigation, anchors, frontmatter, and snippets on the new [Glossary](/glossary) page.
- **15 new dog breed profiles**: Browse profiles for the Chihuahua, Beagle, Dachshund, Labrador Retriever, Poodle, German Shepherd, Bulldog, Rottweiler, Doberman Pinscher, Pug, Shih Tzu, Boxer, Australian Shepherd, Pembroke Welsh Corgi, and Great Dane. Start at [Dogs](/dogs) or open the latest, the [Great Dane](/dogs-2026-11-17-0700).
- **15 new Star Wars guides**: Explore a new guide each day covering factions, starships, planets, and the Force. Start at [Star Wars](/star-wars) or open the latest, [November 17](/star-wars-2026-11-17-1603).

All new pages appear in the sidebar under the Starter Guides anchor.
</Update>
