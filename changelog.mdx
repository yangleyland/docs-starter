<Update label="Nov 21, 2026" tags={["Added"]}>
## Glossary, daily dog breeds, and daily Star Wars pages

### New features

- **Glossary**: Look up core terms like docs.json, navigation, anchors, frontmatter, and snippets in one place. See the [glossary](/glossary).
- **Daily dog breed profiles**: New breed pages ship daily, including [Chihuahua](/dogs-2026-11-03-0701), [Beagle](/dogs-2026-11-04-0700), [Pug](/dogs-2026-11-12-0700), [Corgi](/dogs-2026-11-16-0700), [Great Dane](/dogs-2026-11-17-0700), [Shiba Inu](/dogs-2026-11-20-0700), and [Samoyed](/dogs-2026-11-21-0700). Start from [Types of dogs](/dogs).
- **Daily Star Wars guides**: A new guide to planets, droids, and Sith Lords ships daily. Start from the [Star Wars overview](/star-wars) or read the latest, [Nov 20](/star-wars-2026-11-20-1602).
</Update>
