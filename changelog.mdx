## New breed and Star Wars pages, plus a glossary

### New features

- **Daily dog breed profiles**: Read a new breed profile each day. This week adds the [Great Dane](/dogs-2026-11-17-0700), [Saint Bernard](/dogs-2026-11-18-0700), [Bernese Mountain Dog](/dogs-2026-11-19-0700), [Shiba Inu](/dogs-2026-11-20-0700), [Samoyed](/dogs-2026-11-21-0700), [Akita](/dogs-2026-11-22-0700), and [Alaskan Malamute](/dogs-2026-11-23-0700). Each profile covers temperament, origin, coat, and quick facts. Start from the [Dogs](/dogs) page.
- **Daily Star Wars guides**: Explore a new guide each day covering planets, droids, and Sith Lords. See the [Star Wars](/star-wars) page for the full list, including [Nov 17](/star-wars-2026-11-17-1603) through [Nov 23](/star-wars-2026-11-23-1602).
- **Glossary**: Look up five core terms on the new [Glossary](/glossary) page.

Thanks for reading!
</Update>
