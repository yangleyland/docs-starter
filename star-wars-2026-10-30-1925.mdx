## Canon and Legends

In 2014, Lucasfilm separated Star Wars stories into two continuities.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Canon" icon="check">
    The films, the TV series, and new novels, comics, and games released after April 2014.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Legends" icon="book">
    Expanded Universe stories released before April 2014. These stories are no longer part of the official timeline.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Story formats

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Novels">
    Novels expand on characters and events between and after the films. Canon novels include *Thrawn* by Timothy Zahn.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Comics">
    Comic series follow characters such as Darth Vader and Princess Leia. Marvel Comics publishes the current Canon series.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Video games">
    Games let you play as Jedi, soldiers, and pilots. Some games tell stories that fit into the Canon timeline.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
