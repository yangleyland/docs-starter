Healthy dogs need consistent food, exercise, and training. This page covers the basics.

## Daily care

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Feeding" icon="bowl-food">
    Feed a balanced diet in measured portions at regular times.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exercise" icon="person-walking">
    Walk and play daily. Needs vary by breed, age, and size.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Training" icon="graduation-cap">
    Use short sessions with rewards for good behavior.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Care by life stage

| Stage | Typical age | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Puppy | Up to 1 year | Socialization and basic commands |
| Adult | 1 to 7 years | Steady routine and exercise |
| Senior | 7 years and older | Joint health and regular vet checkups |
