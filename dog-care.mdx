Good care keeps your dog healthy and happy. This page covers the basics.

## Care essentials

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Feeding" icon="bone">
    Feed a balanced diet sized to your dog's age, weight, and activity level.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exercise" icon="person-running">
    Provide daily walks and playtime to burn energy and prevent boredom.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Grooming" icon="scissors">
    Brush the coat, trim nails, and clean ears on a regular schedule.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Care by life stage

| Life stage | Focus | Typical need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Puppy | Socialization and training | Frequent meals and vet visits |
| Adult | Routine and exercise | Daily activity and regular checkups |
| Senior | Comfort and mobility | Joint support and more frequent checkups |
